
EXECUTED TRADES
Double-click an account row to open Executed trades; use Edit account to change registration details. Executed trades is also available inside Analytics. Choose From/To broker dates, then search by account name/number, broker, symbol or ticket and select All, Manual execution, System execution or Classification pending. Press Search executions to apply the filters. Double-click a result for its recorded details.
View, Download and Email produce the same filtered PDF with its reporting range. The screen shows at most 2,000 records to remain manageable; narrow the filters or export the complete matching report. Both scrollbars are available. Retained history is readable when the terminal is offline, but it is not a current broker query. Partial exits are separate records, and separate entry charges stay on entry records. Missing history is not invented.

DAILY LOSS LIMITS
Daily Limits is an account-wide TradePilot entry check. Choose Cash or Percentage, enter a positive loss limit and press Update. Percentage uses the account's starting balance for its broker daily period. Blank means no daily entry limit. Removing a saved limit requires a warning confirmation; changing a limit creates a dated Account changes record in Analytics.
Budget left includes net trading results since the broker-day boundary at 23:30 and all current open floating results, including carried positions. Available broker commission, swap and fee records are included. Deposits and withdrawals are not trading profit. Apply wins and Apply losses affect the daily target; the loss limit always counts both wins and losses.
After the loss threshold is reached, new TradePilot entries stay paused for that broker period even if prices recover. Update the limit deliberately or wait for the next period. Hover over Buy or Sell for the reason. Missing or unverified daily-budget replies block new entries without labelling an otherwise verified broker connection offline.
This check does not close existing positions, cancel broker pending orders or control orders entered outside TradePilot. Pending orders already accepted by the broker can still execute. Stop-loss estimates cannot guarantee the final loss during price gaps, slippage or additional broker charges. Each lead and follower uses its own broker results and remaining budget.
Daily-limit and daily-target messages need enabled email delivery and the relevant account notification preference. Account changes retains dated limit edits with individual references. The desktop and both companions must be the matching version; older companions require a setup refresh.

SPREAD AND ACCURATE TRADE REPORTS
The position sizer Spread ON/OFF control starts OFF. ON moves the planned stop one current spread farther from entry and recalculates size; OFF restores the unadjusted level. Dragging/typing a new stop establishes a new base and applies the spread once. Refreshing never repeatedly adds spread. Existing trade stops are not modified by this planning control. Hover over the control for its purpose.
Activity trade rows include unique TradePilot references; historical CMD references remain readable. Click an attempt to view its exact recorded account, date, symbol, status and response, then View, Download or Email its PDF. A symbol error applies only to that attempt; no fresh quote and broker/local trading restrictions are reported separately. Selection failures without an broker symbol-not-found code are not labelled symbol unavailable.

COUNTRY, PHONE AND CHANGING THE LEAD
Country and phone are mandatory when adding or editing accounts. Select an account owner country from the full country list, then choose the phone country/dialling code and enter the national number. The phone country can differ from the owner country.
To change a follower to lead, Edit account and select Lead account in Account type. Review existing positions first, keep trading off, and close the current lead and the selected follower terminals. Confirm the change. The former lead becomes a follower; both affected setups need refresh through Review terminal setup. Existing trades are not closed or transferred. Pending app commands are cancelled, prior trades are not replayed, payments/history remain recorded, and both role changes appear in account/report audits.

BROKER AUTOMATED TRADING RESTRICTIONS
MT5 error 10026 means the broker server refused automated trading; the toolbar/F7 switches cannot override it. Ask the broker whether this account permits Expert Advisors. Error 10027 is the terminal switch restriction. Review terminal setup reads fresh terminal, advisor and account permission checks separately. A successful connection or enabled local switch does not guarantee the broker will execute an order. Failed entries are not replayed.

ADMIN COMPANY INFORMATION

Admin name, company registration, VAT, tax number, address and logo are optional. Edit account contains account details only. Upload a PNG or JPEG company logo. Blank business fields are omitted. Company logos appear at the top centre and TradePilot at the bottom centre of documents.

MANUAL FOLLOWER CHARTS

It attaches its tools to eligible existing/opened charts while the desktop and terminal are running. Other advisors are preserved; Activity explains conflicts. Matching TradePilot companions that stop reporting can be restarted with their saved inputs and permission flags unchanged.

SEARCH, LEAD HISTORY AND CONNECTION RELIABILITY
Type in any dropdown to narrow the matching choices, then open the arrow list and select a result. Fixed choice fields retain valid selections; typing does not create a new platform or country. Accounts search finds words in names, account numbers, broker, platform, country, email and Paid/Frozen/Due status. Admin guide Search guide words highlights matching text. Activity and Email outbox each have their own word search.
The current lead is always the first account row. Lead count is the number of recorded lead appointments, including the initial lead and later promotions. Retained role audits rebuild previous evidenced appointments; unrecorded history is not invented.
If a follower is offline for more than one minute, TradePilot queues one connection-check email per outage to its saved email and the Admin email. Delivery requires the desktop running, enabled email and a working provider. Reconnection closes the outage. Analytics includes the count of outages over one minute, offline/online times, reference and reason, plus whether live positions were observed around/during the outage. These are desktop-observed times, not proof of executions while disconnected; app-closed gaps and missing history cannot be reconstructed. Connection audit PDFs retain details for each account.

TRADEPILOT — ADMIN GUIDE

Account totals and connection audits
The Accounts header shows Active, Frozen and Due as fractions of registered accounts, followed by Total accounts. Analytics shows one combined summary of all follower accounts, without individual account sheets or an Account report action. Connection audit filters detected offline periods by local desktop date, with a unique ERR ID, first detected time, reconnection time and reported reason. Use Pending diagnosis while the cause is not established; never substitute an invented explanation. Audits are read-only and can be viewed, downloaded or emailed as PDFs. New reports and payment logs include recorded target resets and connection audits. Times represent desktop observations; app-closed gaps cannot be reconstructed.

Start here
Use one demo lead account and one demo follower for your first test. Each account needs its own MetaTrader installation. MT4 and MT5 hedging accounts are supported; MT5 netting accounts are not. Keep copying paused while you set up accounts. Only verified MT4 and MT5 connectors are available; future connectors need their own guided activation process.

1. Update TradePilot
Install the latest approved update through Microsoft Store. Open TradePilot from Start. After an update that changes companion files or startup preparation, use Review terminal setup for the affected account. Releases that change only desktop reports do not need companion reinstall. TradePilot installs the compiled companion; you do not need to find an EA file or press F7 to compile it.

2. Add the lead account account
In Accounts, click Add account. Set Account type to Lead account and choose MT4 or MT5. Enter the account number and broker server exactly as shown in MetaTrader. Enter the requested contact details and use Next. Keep trading permissions disabled in MetaTrader until setup is complete. Enter broker passwords only in MetaTrader. Add account is one seven-step process. Steps 1–3 collect account owner/contact, broker identity and subscription details; step 4 selects the dedicated terminal; step 5 waits for closure and prepares automatically; step 6 opens the terminal and waits for its verified reply; step 7 is a separate trading-permission page. Use Next after entering the required information and Back to review without clearing it or repeating completed preparation. Finish setup may leave trading off. Existing accounts use the Review terminal setup button immediately after Edit account for recovery; it is separate from editing account information. Lead account email and phone, including country code, are required when adding/editing the lead account. Every generated PDF uses the Admin email from Email setup and reminders and the lead account phone number.

3. Connect the lead account
Select the lead account row and click Review terminal setup. Choose the terminal dedicated to that account; check the displayed location if several terminals are installed. Open a chart in MetaTrader and save its profile if no chart can be detected. Only if a chart symbol field appears, enter the exact broker symbol, including its suffix (example: EURUSD.a).

Close only the selected MetaTrader terminal first. TradePilot detects that the selected terminal is closed and automatically installs its companion on every eligible saved chart, preserving chart symbols, timeframes, drawings and other advisors, requests broker-available trade history and saves the connection settings. Wait for the preparation checkmark, then click Open terminal. Log in to the registered broker account if asked. Return to TradePilot and wait for a returned, identity-matched connection check. Sent checks, replies and failures appear in Activity. Click a failed Activity check for its dated error report. OFFLINE in the account table stays a simple status. Follow the activation checklist when you are ready to test. In MT4, enable AutoTrading, select each TradePilot chart, press F7, open Common, check Allow live trading and click OK. In MT5, enable Algo Trading, then use F7 → Common → Allow Algo Trading → OK on each TradePilot chart. Repeat for every TradePilot chart, including new charts. F7 opens advisor properties; no compilation is needed. Leave DLL permissions unchanged. All readiness marks are read-only and update automatically from fresh registered replies, actual advisor permission and follower history/payment. There are no manual permission checkboxes. The single shared connection prefers an available TradePilot chart whose advisor permission you have actually enabled; it does not enable permission for you. Use the setup window scrollbar or Page Down to see the full guide. Future platforms need their own verified activation instructions before their connectors become available.

4. Add and connect each follower
Click Add account and set Account type to Follower account (the follower). Enter the client's name, email, platform, account number and broker server. Choose a whole monthly target from 1% to 100%. Enter the monthly payment amount and currency; use 0 for a free demo test. A payment reference helps match your records. If brokers use different symbol names, fill Different broker symbol names with the exact lead account=follower pair (example: EURUSD=EURUSD.a). Otherwise leave it blank. Use Next with terminal trading permissions disabled and continue the same guided setup using its separate terminal.

TradePilot handles history setup in the background. No I loaded and checked All History checkbox is required. MT5 requests history directly from the broker. MT4 is configured for All History before startup and waits for connected, stable broker-available records. Broker retention can limit older history; the app cannot recreate records a broker no longer supplies. History loading is reported automatically. MT5 followers must use hedging accounts.

5. Use consistent risk settings
The position sizer and copier calculate risk from the current account balance and the distance from entry to stop loss. The lead account records the risk percentage selected in its TradePilot panel; each follower uses that percentage with its own balance and broker contract size. Symbol mappings connect different broker names. The measurer shows chart distances; the basket reports actual trading results.

Commission needs no manual entry and unreported commission does not block setup or sizing. Broker commission, spread, swap and execution differences can make actual results differ from the planned stop-loss price risk. Fees remain included in actual profit/loss and monthly progress; they are not added a second time to the shared sizing calculation.

6. Record the follower's monthly payment
Select the connected follower and click Paid. Its current broker month is read automatically. Choose Paid period starts and 1–60 consecutive months; the full date range and total amount appear before confirmation. Current-month approval resets the balance now. Future months capture the account balance on their first fresh broker connection in that month. Overlapping already-paid future months are rejected without changing records. Payment recorded opens a paid-period receipt with View, Download and Email. Approved periods remain recorded in the payment audit. Enter the payment method and reference, then click Paid after payment is received. Paid renews approval and resets that account’s starting balance to its own fresh broker balance, even while trading permissions are off. History must be ready. The reset receives an audit record; earlier payments, broker trades and paid-period PDFs remain in the historical audit; the main Analytics view shows the combined follower summary. Paid does not change terminal trading permissions. For a free demo test, record a zero payment as paid for that month. This button records payment status; it does not charge the client or collect money. Cancel is always visible at the bottom of the payment window and Escape also closes it without recording payment. The final Paid confirmation can be declined before any record is saved. Each new month needs its own payment record. Download payment reference in the payment window shows the lead account name, broker/server and account number. The recorded payment audit preserves recorded payments by From/To date and view, download or email a branded PDF report with names, account numbers, references, methods and payment status. The PDF includes the TradePilot heading, date period and page numbers. The confusing starting-month-unpaid action has been removed.

7. Start a demo test
Check the lead account and follower account numbers and servers, connection status, complete follower history, the current month's payment. Paid controls approval; Freeze account provides a separate administrative hold. Paid followers are approved automatically for the selected broker month. Enable AutoTrading (MT4) or Algo Trading (MT5) in the correct terminals yourself. Keep both terminals and TradePilot open. Copying is automatic when the lead account is connected with trading permissions enabled and the paid followers are ready.

Existing lead account trades are not copied when you first connect. To test a new copy, place one small new demo trade yourself using the TradePilot trading panel in the lead account terminal and check the follower. Ordinary orders placed outside that panel do not carry TradePilot's captured risk data and are not copied. Check Activity for the result. Close the demo trade yourself in the lead account and verify that the follower closes too. Do not move to live accounts until the demo open, size and close results have been checked.

8. Understand monthly target progress
Every confirmed Paid action captures that follower’s current broker balance as its new starting balance and renews approval for the current broker month. Earlier closed and floating profit or loss is excluded from the new target cycle; historical trades remain in period reports. A later Paid action creates a new reset record rather than deleting the earlier one. Pausing, restarting or reconnecting does not itself reset the starting balance. New deposits are not counted as trading profit.

Example: a current balance of $500 with a 10% target gives a $50 target for subsequent trading profit. A 100% target gives $500. The percentage is a stopping threshold, not a promised return. Progress includes closed trading profit and the change in floating profit since the latest Paid reset. At the target, TradePilot attempts to close positions, remove pending orders and stop new copies for the month. Check the terminal: closed markets or broker rejections can delay closure. A new broker month requires Paid approval and a starting-balance reset from that account’s fresh broker balance.

9. Use the chart tools
Keep the desktop app and connected terminal open. TradePilot automatically attaches its basket manager, position sizer and Point Measurer to every available open chart, including charts opened later. Allow a few seconds per chart. Each chart keeps its symbol, timeframe, indicators and drawings. All panels share one account connection, shared risk settings and basket management; each chart has its own stop-loss line and measurer.

MetaTrader supports one Expert Advisor per chart. If another advisor already occupies a chart, TradePilot keeps that advisor and reports the conflict in Activity. Choose which advisor you want on that chart yourself. Closing the connection chart transfers the shared connection to another TradePilot chart. Trading permissions remain controlled by you in MetaTrader.

The basket groups positions for management; the sizer helps calculate trade size; the measurer helps inspect distances on the chart. Check the symbol, units, size and any proposed action before placing or closing a trade. These tools do not replace checking the broker's order result.

10. Manage clients each day
Use Search to find a client by name or account number. Select a row and click Edit account to change manual client information. Review payment and status columns, Activity for connection or trading messages, and Activity for pending, completed or rejected actions. Reconnect an account after changing connection information. Automatic chart discovery, cost estimates and companion settings run in the background.

Account columns fit their text automatically. Use the bottom scrollbar if the table is wider than the window, and the right scrollbar to move through accounts. Selecting an account shows its complete status and progress below the table, with long messages wrapping to fit the window.

Unpaid followers are switched off in the backend automatically. Each new broker month needs payment approval. To stop automatic copying, disable lead account trading permissions in MetaTrader or close the desktop app. Pausing does not close existing positions. Keep the terminals open to manage existing trades and monthly target closure. Closing TradePilot stops desktop copying; do not assume this closes positions.

11. Freeze or unfreeze an account
Select an account and click Freeze account. Enter the required reason (example: Client requested a temporary break), then click Freeze account to save. TradePilot records the broker date when connected, otherwise the computer date, the account number and a unique FRZ reference. Frozen accounts are red and cannot receive new copied trades. Freezing the lead account stops new copies to all followers. Existing trades and monthly target management continue; freezing does not close trades or change MetaTrader permissions. Select the frozen account and click Unfreeze account, enter a reason and save to remove the hold. Normal payment, connection, history and trading-permission checks still apply; skipped trades are not replayed. Freeze and unfreeze records remain in Activity, the payment log and that broker month's PDF statements. Red also identifies every other inactive account; paid alone does not mean active.

12. Request PDF statements
Select the account and click Statements. Enter its broker calendar month in YYYY-MM (example: 2026-10). Download Entered trades PDF shows entries in that month; Email Entered trades PDF queues it for the configured client/admin recipients. Period-end statement produces exactly two PDFs: Outstanding amount and Closed trades. Choose Download to save both files, or Email to queue both attachments. All reports use the same TradePilot heading, record layout, period and page numbers as the payment report. Outstanding amount shows the recorded subscription fee and payment status for that month. Old months retain the fee recorded when the broker month was first observed. Paid means no outstanding amount; it is an admin approval record, not confirmation from a bank.

Statements use actual broker-available terminal history, loaded and checked automatically for lead accounts and followers. Broker retention may limit earlier records. After upgrading to the statement release, prepare both updated companions with terminals closed and reopen with user-controlled trading permissions off. Incomplete history blocks trade statements; do not treat an empty report as proof when history is incomplete. MT4 records are broker orders; MT5 records are broker deals, including partial exits and reversals. Closed records are selected by broker exit date, so carried-over trades closed this month are included. MT5 entry-side fees remain on entry records. These are account statements, including manual trades, not proof that every trade was copied. Open positions have no final result.

13. End-of-period email
With Send client and admin emails enabled and complete history available, the first fresh connection after the broker month changes queues Outstanding amount and Closed trades for each recorded previous month. The app and terminal must be open; disconnected/offline or incomplete-history periods wait until available. Each account/month is queued once; delivery retries reuse the saved attachments and are visible in Email outbox (click a row for delivery details and saved PDF attachments). No mail is sent when email is disabled. On-request emails can be sent again deliberately. Freeze/unfreeze notes include the reason, date, account number and FRZ ID. Email does not mark a payment or place a trade.

14. Set up optional email reminders
Open Send Mail → Email setup and reminders and enter your admin and sender email addresses. Gmail and Yahoo server, port, encryption and username are detected automatically. Supply your provider app password; for other providers choose Custom provider and use its SMTP instructions. OAuth-only email sign-in is not supported. Save settings and test queues a test only after you enable email. Hover over an input, or focus it with the keyboard, for an example and explanation. Enable Send client and admin emails only when your settings and recipients are correct. Check Email outbox (click a row for delivery details and saved PDF attachments) for delivery status. Email setup is optional; it does not collect payments.

If a status needs attention
• Waiting for terminal: open the configured connection and check the registered login and server.
• Update terminal companion: pause new copying and repeat Review terminal setup after the Store update.
• History loading: keep the registered terminal connected. TradePilot handles the history request and readiness in the background; no manual history confirmation is needed.
• Different broker names: set the exact lead account=follower symbol mapping.
• Inactive (red): check the broker month, payment, history, connection and trading permission. For Frozen, review the recorded reason and unfreeze only when appropriate.
• Rejected trade or delayed closure: read Activity and check the broker's message in MetaTrader.

18. Account performance reports
Analytics has Lead account and Follower account tabs, searchable by name or account number. Each combined summary identifies its selected role and reporting dates. Under Historical statements and payments, choose a named account for broker-month statements; Payment history filters the selected accounts by recorded dates and supports View, Download and Email. Analytics reports use the same structure: account facts and tier, dated balance/equity, verified monthly target progress, broker-month and year-to-date exit results, executed entry/exit record counts, exit-record win rate and profit factor, recorded commissions/swap, open instruments and stop-loss coverage. The selected date range is printed on the report; older history depends on the records retained by the broker and imported by TradePilot. Exit records may include partial closures and do not represent complete round trips. Entry costs are separate; subscription fees use the payment currency. Reports mark cash-flow-adjusted returns, benchmark comparisons, drawdown, volatility and Sharpe ratio unavailable until the required verified data exists. A lead account has no follower tier. Missing broker-deal entry or exit details are labelled Not reported rather than zero. When a terminal is offline, payment shows Month unverified until its broker month can be checked; this does not remove a saved payment.

19. Pending orders and scheduled placement
Pending Order starts OFF on every chart/terminal startup. Turn it ON manually when needed; it never resumes ON automatically. Below Point Measurer, Pending Order uses the standard MT4 types: Buy Limit, Sell Limit, Buy Stop and Sell Stop. The chart supplies the symbol. Choose the type, Volume in broker lots, At price, optional Stop Loss and optional Take Profit (0 omits either protection). Broker lot limits, price steps, minimum distances and trading permissions are checked. A pending order waits for its entry-price condition; placement is not execution.
Place at is optional. Click its calendar and choose a broker date plus hour and minute. Place now is an explicit choice, not a blank. Expiry has its own calendar/time selector and explicit No expiry choice; a selected expiry must be later than placement. Both fields produce a consistent YYYY.MM.DD HH:MM value and cannot be typed as free text. Click Place / Schedule deliberately. A schedule requires this chart and terminal to remain running, connected and trading-enabled. Closing/restarting the chart, changing its timeframe, or losing connection/trading permission cancels the local plan. Placement more than 10 seconds late fails without submitting. TradePilot never silently retries an uncertain broker response or removes an unsupported expiry. Cancel plan affects only the local schedule; manage already-submitted orders in the terminal.
Pending Order has its own ON/OFF switch. OFF cancels any local scheduled placement and hides its inputs while retaining their values; already-submitted broker orders remain unchanged. Point Measurer ON/OFF moves the pending panel below the expanded/collapsed measurer without clearing its inputs or changing the schedule. Schedule is locked against editing until cancelled. The order result appears on the panel (hover for full text) and in the terminal journal. Check the terminal's broker order list for accepted pending orders. Follower accounts accept copied executed positions only: an unfilled lead account pending order is not copied. Once it executes, normal verified copying rules apply.
Analytics and its PDFs show fresh verified broker pending-order details, executed records, wins and losses. Best historical day/hour and hotspots group completed trades by broker entry time, include imported entry/exit costs, and require at least three completed trades per group. MT5 partial exits are combined only when all imported entry volume has closed. Missing entry histories and reversals are excluded from timing comparisons. Positive time groups are ranked by average net result, with sample sizes. These describe imported historical records; they are not predictions or recommendations of future trading times.

ACCOUNT GROUPS, DAILY UPDATES AND BROKER SIZING
Accounts has a separate collapsible section for each named lead strategy, with its own lead and assigned followers. Search filters the account rows and keeps group collapse choices. Current Lead appears after Total accounts. Historical lead appointment counts remain in Analytics, not in the account role column.
Changing a follower to Lead requires two separate confirmations that the current lead terminal and the intended new lead terminal are closed with trading off. TradePilot also checks whether those terminal processes are running. Confirm change promotes the selected account, moves it into Leads, and moves the former lead into Followers. Both affected setups require refreshing; existing positions are not transferred or closed, and earlier trades are not replayed.
Add account searches for terminals and prepares connection files in the background. Chart symbol is detected from saved terminal charts. If there is no saved chart, open a chart in the selected terminal, close it to save the chart, then Retry setup. Do not enter a guessed symbol or alter another account's terminal.
Daily Performance has Apply wins and Apply losses checkboxes beside its target controls. Update saves the entered target and the two selections for the current daily period only. Applied net wins reduce the remaining target; applied net losses increase it. Unchecked results are excluded only from that period's target adjustment, never erased from trade history or reports. A new daily period starts with both selected. Basket uses the same Percentage or Money display mode. Updating the target does not reset actual trade history.
Sizing uses the account's current broker balance, the selected stop-loss distance and broker lot specifications. TradePilot rounds down and limits size to verified available margin. Margin calculation failure is shown as Not verified, never green zero margin. Below minimum lot means the allowed size cannot reach the broker minimum. The final send rechecks sizing and margin; broker acceptance remains required. A margin-limited size risks less than the requested amount. Copied trades use the lead's recorded actual filled risk, not its larger pre-limit request.
Follower accounts can submit user-directed market and pending orders when their broker and terminal permissions allow them. Analytics separates Manual execution and System execution for the selected period, with currencies kept separate. MT5 exit records inherit the entry's broker-reported origin. MT4 uses comments and magic numbers; zero-magic untagged trades are classified as manual. Older records without origin and ambiguous reversals stay Pending. Category totals describe records, including partial exits, rather than guaranteed completed-position counts.
Automatic advance payment reminders are queued individually to each follower's own email address; they do not copy the administrator or other follower addresses. Manual due-account selection also creates a separate message and relevant PDF for each selected follower. Review delivery status in Outbox.


NAMED STRATEGIES AND SIMULTANEOUS LEADS
Each lead has a strategy name. Add a Lead account and name its strategy; when adding a Follower, choose Follow this lead. More than one lead can be connected at the same time. Each expandable strategy section shows the strategy, lead name and broker account number, and Followers: N. Expand its Followers subsection to see only its assigned accounts. Counts include all assigned followers, including frozen or disconnected accounts, and update after adding or reassigning them. Search accepts the strategy name as well as account details. One lead may be assigned all followers.
Copying readiness is independent for each strategy. An unavailable lead blocks its own new copied entries without stopping another ready strategy. New copies use a lead-qualified reference so identical broker ticket numbers on different leads cannot identify the same copied position. Existing legacy copies retain their original references for management. Changing a follower into Lead replaces its assigned lead within that strategy; other leads remain unchanged. Review positions and refresh the two affected setups with trading off before reconnecting. Historical positions are not transferred or replayed.
Example, using two USD accounts and identical contract specifications: a 50,000 lead with a 500 cash risk is risking 1%. A 540 follower receives a 1% budget of 5.40, rather than a 500 cash budget. If loss at the chosen stop is 100 per lot and the lot step is 0.01, 5.40 permits at most 0.05 lots, risking about 5 before separately reported fees. A broker minimum of 0.10 lots would prevent this entry rather than round it up. Margin checks can reduce size further or reject it. Different broker contracts, stop prices and account currencies require their own broker valuation; equal percentages do not imply equal lots. These are calculation examples, not promised trading results.
The expanded lead/follower analytics selector, daily limits, local app lock and notifications are included in the development candidate. The build must complete every remaining validation before the Microsoft update is submitted.


LOCAL ACCESS, COMPANY INFORMATION AND AUDIT REFERENCES
The first launch asks you to create a local sign-in name and password of at least 10 characters. Broker passwords stay in the terminal. On later launches enter your local name and password to unlock the admin screen. The name can be remembered; the password itself is not saved as readable text. Lock app hides the admin screen and existing report windows while the controller continues. Unlock restores those windows. Local access lets you choose 5, 15, 30 or 60 minutes of inactivity, or Never. Saving access settings requires your current password; a new password must be confirmed. Keep the password because TradePilot cannot retrieve it. Closing the app and forced shutdown have different consequences from locking: local copying, schedules and delivery stop when the app closes.
Add account includes company/account holder information for each lead. The company/account holder name is required; optional registration, VAT, tax, address and logo fields can be left blank. Edit account can update that lead's information later. A follower's PDF identifies its assigned lead and strategy, including that lead's company/logo. Combined reports list the included lead strategies rather than substituting the first lead. Earlier company details remain associated with the original lead. Company edits have dated audit records; blank optional details are omitted.
Account ID is a separate TradePilot identifier, not the broker login number. A new installation's sequence starts at 00000000000001. New accounts, freezes, account/company edits, connection errors and payment record IDs use fourteen-digit references. Each issued reference is unique within that installation. Existing historical references remain unchanged. Payment records keep the entered bank/payment reference alongside the generated TradePilot record ID. Internal broker trade tickets and connection credentials are retained for their original purpose; they are not account IDs.
Analytics reports include concise retained audit counts and the latest edit reference. Account changes opens the dated change/freeze details and their own View, Download or Email PDF. Connection reliability opens dated outages, observed live-trade coverage, reconnection times and reported reasons, also with its own PDF. These records reflect retained observations, not reconstructed activity while the desktop was closed. The selected date range appears on each report.

EMAIL PREFERENCES AND DESKTOP POWER
Edit account includes Automatic email notifications for connection interruptions, target reached/closure, trade request errors and daily loss limits. Turning an email category off does not remove Activity records or disable safety checks. Subscription payment notices are separate and remain individual to the relevant follower. Daily loss limit reached has its own notification preference. Daily target notifications use the target preference. Stop-loss and closed-loss email preferences are separate. MT5 stop-loss notifications use the broker-reported execution reason, including profitable trailing stops. MT4 history does not supply a definitive closure reason, so its loss emails say Closed trade loss without guessing stop-loss execution. The first complete history import establishes a baseline silently; old trades are not replayed as new alerts. A general trade request error is not confirmation of a stop-loss execution.
Unexpected offline alerts apply independently to leads and eligible paid followers after 1, 15 and 30 minutes, then stop for that outage. Frozen and unpaid follower accounts are excluded. A fresh return reply closes the detected outage; a later outage gets a new reference. SMTP delivery still requires a configured sender and enabled email delivery; Outbox shows delivery status.
When a battery-powered desktop is unplugged and reaches 40% or lower, TradePilot records a warning and queues one administrator email if email delivery is enabled. It resets this warning after external power returns or charge rises above 40%. Existing broker orders are not closed by the warning. Keep the desktop and terminal running for new copies, locally scheduled placements and email delivery; Windows can force shutdown before an application can display its warning.

DAILY TARGETS AND SEPARATE BASKETS
Ratio is the requested reward divided by the saved position-sizer risk; 1:2 means a target of twice that risk. Cash and Percentage targets remain available. Use Update to save a new target and its Apply wins/Apply losses choices. Ratio is a planning target, not a guaranteed result.
Daily daily loss limit is part of Daily Performance. One neutral-coloured Update button is the last control in Daily Performance and saves the target, Apply wins, Apply losses and daily loss limit together. Before saving, the confirmation shows Apply wins and Apply losses as ON/OFF, then Continue? with Yes and No. Cancelling saves nothing from that Update action. Enter valid values before saving; invalid input or cancelling removal of a loss limit prevents the save. Apply wins and Apply losses default to OFF for a new daily period. Hover over either for its purpose; press Update to save your selections for that period. Stateful buttons show green when ON and red when OFF, with text indicating the state. Update saves your choices; it is an action rather than an ON/OFF switch.

Daily target progress and Basket Manager use positions belonging to their original broker period, starting at 23:30. Carried positions keep their older baskets. Click the Carry-over summary to view older open positions, grouped by original basket date and ordered by entry time; Back and Next choose basket dates in Progress. Trades opens that basket’s individual entry dates, symbols and broker tickets, with six entries per page; Back and Next then choose trade-entry pages. Progress returns to the same basket summary. Hover over an entry for its full details. Closing the list does not close trades. Each older basket closes when its own net target is reached and trading is permitted, rather than at a guaranteed clock time. Reaching today's target does not close yesterday's positions. The daily loss entry limit remains account-wide: it includes all open results and today's realised results, regardless of basket.
Point Measurer has its own Spread ON/OFF control. ON adds one fresh spread to the measuring stop; OFF restores its base. This is independent of the position sizer and does not amend accepted broker orders. Reports label current chart measurements separately from historic execution costs.
SELECTED ANALYTICS AND EXECUTIONS
Choose the date range, then the Lead or Follower tab and one, several or all accounts. Search filters the visible choices; hidden checked accounts remain selected. Reports combine the selected accounts with separate currency totals. Double-click an account row to search its retained Executed trades by symbol, name, broker number, ticket or Manual/System category. View, Download and Email use PDF.
Completed-position statistics include retained entry and exit costs. MT5 positions with missing entry records, remaining volume or reversals are excluded from complete-position outcomes. The cumulative results chart starts at zero for the selected range and is cash trading results, not an equity curve or investor return. Maximum daily closed-result drawdown uses day-end results; it is not intraday account drawdown.

TERMINAL KEY REFRESH AND NET TARGETS
Review terminal setup refreshes the selected MT5 account's existing TradePilot chart inputs while its terminal is closed. It preserves chart layouts, other advisors and saved trading permission flags. The shared authenticated owner publishes the account-wide daily reply; other charts keep their interactive tools.
A daily target is a net trading-result target. Reported commission/fees and swap are included once. Bid/ask execution spread already affects broker price profit; adding it again to reported net profit would double-count it. A 500 net target therefore requires sufficient price profit to cover reported costs as well as retain 500. Missing historic spread data is not reconstructed from today's quotes.
Lead appointment From–To dates follow retained account role records. Current appointments say Current appointment; a missing end date is disclosed as End date not retained. These are recorded account times, not reconstructed broker executions.


ANALYTICS CHARTS
Selected Lead and Follower PDF summaries include cumulative completed net results, daily completed wins/losses and buy/sell outcomes, instrument net-result rankings with sample counts, and historical entry-time hotspots. Date ranges follow final exits; trade direction comes from entry records. Costs retained at entry and exit are included once. Different currencies stay separate. Flat outcomes are neither wins nor losses; missing direction stays unclassified. Hotspots require at least three outcomes to show a coloured average and are historical observations, not trading promises. Current balance/equity snapshots are not reconstructed historic account values.


TERMINAL COLUMN LAYOUT AND PENDING FILLS
The main column contains Daily Performance and Position Sizer. Current Basket and Carry-over occupy the middle column. Point Measurer and Pending Order occupy the right column. Automatic fitting reserves space for all three columns. Pending orders are broker orders, not open positions until the broker confirms a fill. Filled volume appears in the basket belonging to its broker entry date, and TradePilot pending fills are classified as System Manual Execution in retained history and analytics. Broker acceptance alone is never reported as execution. The Pending Order status follows the most recently submitted ticket on that chart during the current chart session; broker history and account analytics retain previous tickets. Older baskets remain separate. Turning Pending Order OFF only cancels an unsent local schedule; existing broker orders remain in force.

Remaining Target in Current Basket shows the selected mode next to the heading, with the other two cash, percentage or risk-to-reward equivalents on separate lines below. Percentage uses the original daily starting balance. Reward ratio uses the saved planned risk for Ratio mode, or current configured planned risk for the other modes. If a positive risk amount is unavailable it says Ratio pending. These are equivalent views of the remaining target, not three separate targets. Hover shows the full value and calculation basis.


BASKET COSTS AND SPREAD
Commission = and Swap = separately show broker-reported amounts for the original-day basket, including currently accrued swap. Credits appear as negative charges. Separately reported broker fees remain included once in net results and are disclosed on hover and in carry-over details. Spread = shows a fresh current bid/ask estimate for remaining open volume using that broker's contract; it is not a claim about the spread paid on entry. Gross estimate illustrates net target plus reported costs plus current spread, for example 500 + 3 + 2 = 505. The actual close test keeps the net target at 500 because reported net results already include charges and executable broker profit already reflects spread. Never add them twice. Future exit charges and unallocated account-wide charges are not invented. After exits, entry quotes are required to establish an historic gross/spread equivalent; current quotes cannot reconstruct it. Carry-over baskets retain their own targets and costs.
When MT5 retains no active-profile marker, setup can suggest symbols from its saved Default profile. These are saved-profile suggestions, not confirmation of currently open charts; fresh platform replies remain required.


CARRY-OVER PROGRESS
Click Carry-over to view each original broker-day basket separately; Back and Next select other basket dates. Each page shows the original date, first entry, open position count, saved net target, verified net profit/loss, remaining target, progress percentage, commission, swap, separately reported fees and current spread estimate. Progress and remaining target follow that original period’s saved Apply wins/losses choices. Net profit/loss always shows the actual broker result, so it can differ from counted progress. Each basket closes only at its own net target when trading permissions and valid broker conditions allow; today’s basket cannot close older baskets. An empty list means there are no older open baskets. No trades are changed by opening or closing this view.


BASKET ROWS AND DISPLAY MODES
Positions is the number of open trades. Floating trades shows open broker price profit/loss before separately reported commission and swap. Costs estimate combines reported original-day charges with a fresh current open-volume spread estimate, without claiming historical entry spread or future fees. Commission, Swap and Spread remain separate breakdowns. Floating P/L uses the saved target mode (Cash, Percentage or Ratio), with the other two equivalents in brackets. Percentage uses original daily starting balance; Ratio uses planned cash risk. Total P/L is the original-day basket’s combined closed and open net broker results; charges are counted once and estimates are not subtracted again. The remaining target and status follow these result rows.


DAILY UPDATE CONFIRMATION
Update opens an in-chart confirmation showing Apply wins, Apply losses and Continue? Choose Yes to save the reviewed settings or No to cancel. Platform replies and background monitoring continue while this prompt is open. If removing an existing loss limit, the prompt also identifies that removal. If the broker period or reviewed inputs change before Yes, review them and press Update again. Confirmation does not grant trading permission.


TERMINAL HEADER AND STARTUP
Each terminal panel shows TradePilot, By Tinashe Chimanikire and Trade management together in the top header; the lower signature and Point Measurer author labels are removed. MT4 queues chart initialization until the terminal finishes loading its saved charts. Setup and cleanup preserve broker orders and trading permission flags. A frozen terminal is not a verified connection; Activity requires an actual fresh reply.


COMPILED COMPANION DOWNLOAD
In Analytics, Terminal setup files lets you download one ZIP containing MT4/TradePilot.ex4, MT5/TradePilot.ex5 and Setup-guide.pdf. The bundle excludes source code, account credentials, connection settings and databases. Existing downloads are preserved: choose a new filename to save another copy. MARGIN INDICATORS AND SAVED PROFILES
A green Required Margin value means a positive calculated volume passed the current broker margin check. Below minimum lot, an unavailable calculation or an unaffordable volume must not display green. Refresh the calculation after prices or account funds change; a displayed estimate is not an execution guarantee.
An MT4 TradePilot profile stores the selected account’s startup chart layout and companion attachments so they can recover after reopening. It is not another trading account. Original chart profiles are retained. MT5 setup uses its existing saved charts, an account-specific preset and startup configuration; it does not require a newly generated chart profile. A TradePilot-named MT5 profile may be a retained older setup or a user-saved layout. Do not delete a currently selected recovery profile blindly: confirm ownership and the replacement restart behavior first.
Carry-over Progress and Trades are separate read-only views. If there are no older open baskets, Trades explicitly explains there are no older open entries; today’s positions remain in Current Basket. Closing either view never closes positions.


LEAD TRADE ACTIVATION NOTICES
After the first verified connection, a newly observed lead trade with reported trading permission OFF produces a dated Activity warning and an administrator notice under Trade request errors preferences. The notice identifies the lead, strategy, broker, symbol, ticket, verified account condition and reference. It does not change permissions, place or close orders, or replay the trade after activation. Existing positions on first connection do not produce these notices.
A new order without a valid stop or captured TradePilot risk has a separate Signal skipped notice explaining what to review, including the position sizer and F7 on each intended chart. External manual broker orders do not reliably identify their originating chart; TradePilot must not claim a particular unactivated chart caused the order. Check each intended chart as instructed. Account-level notices are verified in isolated tests; exact chart attribution and native tests remain pending.

LABEL DESCRIPTIONS
Hover over panel labels and section headings for their purpose and displayed units. Desktop descriptions disappear when you leave, click or scroll. Terminal tooltips follow MetaTrader hover behavior. A description does not change a value or grant trading permission.

SYSTEM MANUAL EXECUTION
Trades you place with TradePilot Buy, Sell or Pending Order controls appear as System Manual Execution. Trades placed directly in MT4/MT5 remain Manual execution. Copied trades remain System execution. These categories describe how a trade was placed, not whether it made a profit. Exit records keep the entry category when it can be verified; older records without enough detail remain Pending. The updated desktop and companions are required together for the new category.

TARGET AND BUDGET EQUIVALENTS
Daily Performance shows the saved target in the selected display mode, with the other two equivalents below. Cash uses the connected account currency, percentage uses the daily starting balance, and ratio uses planned cash risk. Choose a mode, enter a target and press Update to save it; choosing the mode alone does not change the active basket target. Budget Left shows the cash loss allowance plus percentage and risk units. One risk unit is the cash risk entered in Position Sizer; it is not a profit promise. A ZAR account shows ZAR, a USD account shows USD and a GBP account shows GBP, even when the chart symbol uses other currencies. Pending means the needed balance or risk is not yet available.

PANEL CONTROLS AND LAYOUT
New ON/OFF controls start OFF in red; ON is green. Saved MT4 stop-line choices remain saved. Calculate displays whether spread allowance is ON or OFF; it does not place a trade. The Daily Loss Limit rows separate mode, limit and remaining budget. Current Basket Total P/L has an outline and includes broker net results once. Point Measurer and Pending Order have matching compact OFF headings.

CURRENT BASKET OUTCOMES
Wins and Losses count completed trades from the basket's original broker day using net results after reported charges. Open trades stay under Positions. Break-even trades are neither wins nor losses. MT5 combines deals for each fully closed position; MT4 counts closed broker tickets. Apply wins/losses changes target progress, not these actual outcome counts. Labels and the right-hand value column both read left to right.

BASKET DISPLAY MODES
Current Basket follows the selected Daily Performance Cash, Percentage or Ratio display immediately, without changing the saved cash target or entry limits. Press Update to save target settings. Wins and Losses appear above bordered Total P/L. Daily Loss Limit Budget left shows unbracketed account cash; its bracketed percentage and R equivalent align in the value column. One R means the planned cash risk in Position Sizer.

WIN AND LOSS AMOUNTS
Each outcome shows its closed net amount in the selected Cash, Percentage or Ratio mode, the other two equivalents in brackets below, and a separate closed-trade counter beneath. Hover any of these fields for an explanation. Open trades are not completed wins or losses.

DAILY LOSS LIMIT AND CHART DISPLAY
Daily Loss Limit replaces the Daily Loss Limit heading. Remaining target is green for verified closed profit, red for verified closed loss, and neutral when flat or awaiting verification. Limit mode and loss-limit entries use the common value column. Current Basket omits Gross estimate and Costs estimate; verified Commission, Swap and Spread remain separately explained. Floating P/L appears directly above Total P/L with a lighter outline. Position Sizer retains the risk input and all sizing/margin checks but removes the duplicate Risk Amount row. Money and required margin use the broker account deposit currency, never an instrument's name. Carry-over floating P/L shows account cash with a percentage of distinct original-day starting balances; this is not an investment return. Spread OFF Buy/Sell opens Continue/Cancel for one order; Continue rechecks broker data and permissions, Cancel sends nothing. Wins and Losses show their net amounts and mode equivalents; the shared Trade count beneath them reads wins/losses (W/L), for example 1/2 (W/L). Hover fields and controls for explanations.

CALCULATE WITH SPREAD OFF
Calculate opens an in-chart Continue/Cancel notice when Spread is OFF. Continue calculates using your entered stop without an extra spread allowance; it does not place a trade. Cancel leaves the calculation unchanged. Buy/Sell uses a separate trade confirmation and rechecks normal broker safeguards. Required margin uses the broker account's deposit currency. Win/loss mode equivalents sit beside their main amounts; the shared W/L counter remains a separate row. Carry-over and action buttons use the compact row layout. Daily Loss Limit status is green when its entry guard allows entries and red when that guard pauses them; terminal trading permission is still a separate requirement.


REPORT TOTALS AND CARRY-OVER BALANCES
Carry-over always keeps its known cash result and broker account currency visible. If the original balance is not available, Percentage pending appears beside that amount until the balance can be verified. Analytics with no completed trades shows zero Wins, zero Losses and a 0/0 (W/L) trade count. These zeros describe completed outcomes only; they do not imply there are no open positions.
